# Retracoon privacy policy

_Last updated: fill in the date you publish this. Contact: fill in your email address._

Retracoon is a game. It has no accounts and no chat, and it does not ask for your name, email or location.

## What stays on your device

Your progress (best runs, Bottle Caps, Gems, owned skins, settings) is saved on your device only. Deleting the app deletes it.

## Ads

The free version shows one full-screen ad when the game starts, served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address and basic device information to show and measure ads. In the EU and UK you are asked for consent first. On iOS you are asked whether apps may track you; if you say no, ads are not personalised. Buying **No Ads** removes the ad.

Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

## Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to check that a purchase is valid and to restore it on a new device; RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user id and your purchase receipts.

RevenueCat's policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

## Children

Retracoon is not directed at children under 13.
