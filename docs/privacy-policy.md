# Retracoon privacy policy

_Last updated: fill in the date you publish this. Contact: fill in your email address._

Retracoon is a game. It has no accounts, logins or chat, and it does not ask for your real name, email or location.

## What stays on your device

Your progress (best runs, Bottle Caps, Gems, owned skins, settings) is saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes it. Your best scores are also sent to the scoreboard, see below.

## Scoreboard

When the game is online it joins the scoreboard under a random player id and a nickname (a random one, or one you choose). Our server stores that nickname, your best stage, level and distance, the skin you wear, your friend list (players whose friend code you entered, or who entered yours) and when you last played. Other players can see your nickname, skin and scores; only you see your friend code. The server sees your IP address to limit abuse but does not store it with your scores.

To delete your scoreboard data, email the contact above with your nickname.

## Ads

The free version shows one full-screen ad when the game starts, served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address and basic device information to show and measure ads. In the EU and UK you are asked for consent first. On iOS you are asked whether apps may track you; if you say no, ads are not personalised. Buying **No Ads** removes the ad.

Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

## Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to check that a purchase is valid and to restore it on a new device; RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user id and your purchase receipts.

RevenueCat's policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

## Children

Retracoon is not directed at children under 13.
