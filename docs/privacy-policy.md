# Retracoon privacy policy

_Last updated: fill in the date you publish this. Contact: fill in your email address._

Retracoon is a game. You sign in with Google, Apple or Steam so your name, scores and purchases belong to you. It has no chat, and it does not ask for your real name, email or location.

## What stays on your device

Your progress (best runs, Bottle Caps, Gems, owned skins, settings) is saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes it. Your best scores are also sent to the scoreboard, see below.

## Your account

When you sign in, Google, Apple or Steam tells our server a stable account id for you (not your password, and we don't ask for your email). Our server stores that account id, a random player id, your nickname with its 4-digit tag (for example SNEAKY BANDIT#1234), your best stage, level and distance, the skin you wear, your friend list (players whose friend code you entered, or who entered yours), your gem balance, skins bought with gems, a record of your purchases (product and store transaction id) and when you last played. If you sign in with Apple we also keep a token from Apple so we can cancel it when you delete your account.

Other players can see your nickname with its tag, skin and scores; only you see your friend code and gems. The server sees your IP address to limit abuse but does not store it with your account.

**Deleting your account:** in the game open Scores > Account > Delete account. This deletes everything listed above right away. You can also follow the steps at https://YOUR-API/account/delete.

## Ads

The free version shows one full-screen ad when the game starts, served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address and basic device information to show and measure ads. In the EU and UK you are asked for consent first. On iOS you are asked whether apps may track you; if you say no, ads are not personalised. Buying **No Ads** removes the ad.

Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

## Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to check that a purchase is valid; RevenueCat receives your random player id and your purchase receipts, and tells our server what you bought so it can be added to your account. The Steam version has no in-game purchases.

RevenueCat's policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

## Children

Retracoon is not directed at children under 13.
